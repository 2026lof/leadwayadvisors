High-Income Professionals

A high salary should not mean no options.

Wage income is the hardest income to shelter, which is exactly why planning matters. We look at compensation, investments, side income and giving together, so each decision is made with the tax cost in view.

Strategies

Strategies we use with high earners.

Equity compensation

RSUs, stock options and employee purchase plans: when to exercise, when to sell and how to avoid surprises from withholding shortfalls and the alternative minimum tax.

Retirement and Roth strategies

Getting the most from workplace plans, backdoor and mega backdoor Roth contributions where your plan allows, and choosing between pre-tax and Roth each year.

Side income and consulting

Consulting, board or locum income opens business deductions and a Solo 401(k) that a salary alone does not.

Charitable planning

Donor-advised funds and gifts of appreciated shares, with giving bunched into the years where the deduction is worth the most.

Investment tax management

Tax-loss harvesting, placing assets in the right type of account and planning around the net investment income tax.

Real estate as a tax strategy

For some households, a rental that one spouse actively manages changes how losses are treated. We test whether it works for you first.

Which of these apply depends on your facts. Tax rules change and many have income limits, holding requirements or state differences, so each strategy is confirmed against your situation before it goes into your plan.

Beyond the Return

Year-round, not once a year.

  • Withholding check-ups. Paycheck and bonus withholding reviewed so there is no April shortfall or penalty.
  • Quarterly estimates. Payments calculated when investment or side income makes them necessary.
  • Multi-state income. Remote work, relocations and deferred compensation allocated correctly between states.
  • Life events. Home purchases, marriage, new jobs and liquidity events planned before they happen.
  • Cross-border matters. Overseas assignments, foreign accounts and non-US spouses handled alongside your US return.
