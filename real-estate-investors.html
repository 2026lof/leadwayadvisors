Real Estate Investors

Keep more of what your properties earn.

Real estate is one of the most tax-favoured assets there is, but only if the rules are used on purpose. We plan acquisitions, operations and exits so depreciation, losses and gains work in your favour.

Strategies

Strategies we use with investors.

Cost segregation

An engineering-based study that separates a building into components with shorter lives, moving a large share of depreciation into the early years of ownership.

Bonus depreciation

Qualifying components of property acquired after January 19, 2025 can be written off in full in the first year. We time purchases and studies to make the most of it.

Short-term rental rules

Where the average guest stay is seven days or less and you materially participate, losses may offset wages and other active income instead of being suspended.

Real estate professional status

For investors who qualify, rental losses are no longer passive. We help you document hours and participation so the position holds up.

1031 exchanges

Deferring gain by rolling sale proceeds into replacement property, with the identification and closing deadlines planned before you list.

Entity and ownership structure

LLCs, partnerships and holding structures chosen for liability protection, financing and tax, across every state you own in.

Which of these apply depends on your facts. Tax rules change and many have income limits, holding requirements or state differences, so each strategy is confirmed against your situation before it goes into your plan.

Beyond the Return

The work that keeps the strategy standing.

  • Per-property bookkeeping. Income, expenses and basis tracked for each property, so every return and sale is supported.
  • Multi-state filing. Returns in each state where you hold property, with credits claimed to avoid double tax.
  • Passive loss tracking. Suspended losses carried forward and released when a property is sold.
  • Sale planning. Depreciation recapture and capital gains modelled before you accept an offer.
  • Quarterly estimates. Payments adjusted as you buy, refinance and sell during the year.
