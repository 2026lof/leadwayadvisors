How this is calculated. The estimator uses 2026 federal income tax brackets and the standard deduction. The S-corporation figure compares self-employment tax on your full profit with payroll tax on a salary of 40% to 60% of profit. The cost segregation figure assumes the building is 80% of the purchase price and that 20% to 30% of it qualifies for first-year depreciation. The retirement figure assumes an employee deferral of up to $24,500. Strategy totals can be lower than the sum of the lines because deductions stack.

Important. This is an illustration, not tax advice. It ignores state taxes, the pass-through deduction, passive loss limits, phase-outs, other income taxed outside the brackets and the cost of implementing each strategy. Rental losses can only offset other income in certain circumstances, and retirement contributions and accelerated depreciation defer tax; they do not eliminate it.
