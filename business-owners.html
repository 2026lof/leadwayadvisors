Business Owners

Structure the business so less of the profit goes to tax.

Most owners overpay because their structure, payroll and books were set up once and never revisited. We review how the business is built, then keep it tuned through the year.

Strategies

Strategies we use with owners.

Entity selection

Sole proprietorship, LLC, S-corporation or C-corporation: we compare the tax cost of each for your profit level and plans, and handle the change if one is worth making.

S-corporation planning

Paying yourself a reasonable salary and taking the rest as distributions can reduce self-employment tax. We set the salary, run the numbers and keep it defensible.

Pass-through deduction

The qualified business income deduction can remove up to 20% of pass-through profit from tax. We manage wages and income so you stay within its limits.

State tax elections

Many states let pass-through businesses pay state tax at the entity level, turning a capped personal deduction into a full business deduction.

Retirement plans

Solo 401(k), SEP and cash balance plans that shelter far more than an IRA, matched to your profit and whether you have staff.

Reimbursement policies

Accountable plans for home office, vehicle and travel costs, so legitimate business expenses are deducted properly and documented.

Which of these apply depends on your facts. Tax rules change and many have income limits, holding requirements or state differences, so each strategy is confirmed against your situation before it goes into your plan.
