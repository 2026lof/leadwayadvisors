Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Leadway Advisors (“we”, “us”) collects through leadwayadvisors.com, how we use it and the choices you have. It covers this website only. Information you give us as a client is also governed by your engagement agreement and the professional confidentiality rules that apply to us.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact form we collect your name, email address, phone number if you provide it, the type of client you are and your message.
  • Consultation bookings. Bookings are made through Calendly, which collects the details you enter there under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider records standard server data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver and secure the site.

The savings estimator runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are not sent to us or stored.

Please do not send tax documents, Social Security numbers or account details through the contact form. We will give you a secure way to share them once we are working together.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange consultations.
  • To provide and improve our services and this website.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.
  • To meet our legal and professional obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site and respond to you, including Netlify (website hosting and form handling), Calendly (scheduling) and Google Fonts (typefaces, which involves your browser requesting font files from Google). We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Third-party services you choose to use from the site, such as Calendly, may set their own.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our dealings with you, and for longer where the law or our professional obligations require.

Your choices

You can ask us to give you a copy of, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you from this website. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local privacy law. To make a request, email contact@leadwayadvisors.com.

Children

This website is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
