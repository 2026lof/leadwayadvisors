Our Bookkeeping Standard

Every month closed against a documented checklist.

Good tax strategy depends on good books. Each client’s month is worked through the same checklist, from transaction categorization to a reviewed balance sheet, and a month is not treated as finished until every step is complete.

What that means for you

  • Consistent close. The same steps, in the same order, every month.
  • A clear record. Each step is logged with who completed it and when.
  • Sales tax built in. Sales tax returns are tracked on the same checklist where they apply.
  • Tax-ready books. Planning and filing start from numbers that have already been reviewed.

Cross-Border

One firm for income, businesses and property in more than one country.

Living in one country and earning in another usually means two tax systems, two sets of deadlines and a real risk of paying twice. With teams covering the US, UK and Australia, we plan both sides together.

See key US, UK and Australian tax dates →

  • US citizens and green card holders abroad. US returns are still required wherever you live; we handle them alongside your local filing.
  • Avoiding double tax. Foreign tax credits, the foreign earned income exclusion and treaty positions, applied deliberately.
  • Foreign account reporting. FBAR and FATCA disclosures for overseas bank, pension and investment accounts.
  • Businesses expanding across borders. Entity structure, VAT or GST registration and bookkeeping in each country.
  • Overseas owners of US property. Rental income reporting, withholding and the right ownership structure.
