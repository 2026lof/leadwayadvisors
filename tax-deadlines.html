Tax Deadlines

Key tax dates in the US, UK and Australia.

The recurring dates most individuals and small businesses need to plan around, in one place.

United States

Federal deadlines

For calendar-year taxpayers. State deadlines often match but not always.

DateWhat is due
January 15Fourth-quarter estimated tax payment for the previous year
January 31W-2s to employees and 1099-NEC forms to contractors
March 15Partnership (Form 1065) and S-corporation (Form 1120-S) returns, or extension
April 15Individual returns (Form 1040) and calendar-year C-corporation returns, or extension. First-quarter estimated payment. FBAR foreign account report
June 15Second-quarter estimated payment. Filing date for US citizens living abroad
September 15Third-quarter estimated payment. Extended partnership and S-corporation returns
October 15Extended individual and C-corporation returns. Extended FBAR
United Kingdom

HMRC deadlines

The UK tax year runs from April 6 to April 5.

DateWhat is due
January 31Online Self Assessment return for the previous tax year, any balance owed, and the first payment on account
April 5End of the tax year
April 6Start of the new tax year
July 31Second payment on account
October 5Register for Self Assessment if you have new untaxed income
October 31Paper Self Assessment returns
QuarterlyVAT returns and payment, due one month and seven days after the end of each VAT period
Company year endCorporation Tax is payable nine months and one day after the accounting period ends; the return is due within twelve months
Australia

ATO deadlines

The Australian financial year runs from July 1 to June 30.

DateWhat is due
June 30End of the financial year
July 1Start of the new financial year
July 28Quarterly BAS and PAYG instalment for April to June
October 28Quarterly BAS and PAYG instalment for July to September
October 31Individual tax returns, if you lodge your own. Later dates may apply through a registered tax agent
February 28Quarterly BAS and PAYG instalment for October to December
March 31End of the fringe benefits tax year
April 28Quarterly BAS and PAYG instalment for January to March
May 21Fringe benefits tax return and payment, if you lodge your own

This is a general guide to recurring dates, not a complete list. When a date falls on a weekend or public holiday it usually moves to the next business day, and different dates apply to fiscal-year businesses, monthly filers and returns lodged through an agent. Confirm the dates that apply to you with your advisor.
